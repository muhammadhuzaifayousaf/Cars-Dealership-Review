About Our Dealership Platform

Welcome to our car dealership platform. Explore dealerships across different states, browse available car makes and models, and read customer reviews to make informed decisions.

This project combines a React frontend and Django application with supporting APIs and database services to provide dealership information and customer review features.

Explore Dealerships

Browse dealership details and filter results by state.

Customer Reviews

Read dealership feedback and explore review sentiment.

Car Information

Explore car makes and models to support your research.
